Motor Insurance

Premium rates vary based on vehicle class and coverage type:

  • Private Cars: Comprehensive: 3.5% (claims-free), 4% (with claim record); TPFT: 2% + TPP; TPO: 100,000 TZS
  • Motorcycles (Two-wheelers): Comprehensive: 5% (claims-free), 6% (with claim record); TPFT: 3% + TPP; TPO: 50,000 TZS
  • Motorcycles (Three-wheelers): Comprehensive: 6% (claims-free), 7% (with claim record); TPFT: 3% + TPP; TPO: 75,000 TZS
  • Commercial Vehicles: General Goods Carrying: 4% (claims-free), 4.75% (with claim record); TPFT: 2.5% + TPP; TPO: 500,000 TZS
  • Trailers: Comprehensive: 4% (claims-free), 4.75% (with claim record); TPO: 100,000 TZS
  • Oil Tankers: Comprehensive: 6% (steel tankers below 10 years), 7% (aluminium tankers below 10 years); TPFT: 4% + TPP; TPO: 750,000 TZS
  • Passenger Carrying Vehicles: Public Taxis/Private Hire: 5% + 15,000 TZS per seat (claims-free), 6% (with claim record); TPO: 500,000 TZS
  • Buses: Daladala (within city): 8% + 15,000 TZS per seat; Up Country: 8% + 30,000 TZS per seat; Private: 5% + 10,000 TZS per seat; School: 5% + 7,500 TZS per seat
